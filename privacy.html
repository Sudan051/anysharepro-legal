Privacy Policy

Last Updated: November 19, 2026 • Effective Date: November 19, 2026

Core Privacy Guarantee: Data Not Collected

AnySharePro is an offline, peer-to-peer (P2P) file transfer utility. It does not upload your files, metadata, device details, or contact information to any remote server or cloud service. Every single byte is transferred directly between your devices over your private local network.

1. Overview & Developer Information

AnySharePro is created, owned, and maintained by independent developer Saurabh Kumar Sharma ("we", "our", or "us"). We believe privacy is an absolute human right. AnySharePro was deliberately architected without telemetry trackers, cloud relays, user accounts, or analytics scripts.

2. What We Do NOT Collect

Our infrastructure collects zero user data. Specifically:

  • No Personal Identifiers: We do not ask for or collect names, email addresses, phone numbers, usernames, or passwords.
  • No File Contents or Metadata: Your photos, videos, audio clips, archives, and documents are never viewed, indexed, copied, or stored on remote servers.
  • No Advertising or Tracking Identifiers: We do not track IDFA (Identifier for Advertisers), Google Advertising ID, or cross-app browsing habits.
  • No Analytics SDKs: No third-party analytical SDKs (such as Firebase Analytics, Mixpanel, AppsFlyer, or Facebook SDK) are embedded in AnySharePro.

3. Device Permissions & How They Are Used

AnySharePro requests only the minimum iOS/Android system permissions strictly necessary to establish local transfers:

  • Local Network Permission (iOS 14+): Used exclusively to discover other nearby devices on the same Wi-Fi subnet or Personal Hotspot via Bonjour/mDNS protocols and to establish direct TCP socket streams.
  • Camera Permission: Used exclusively to scan the temporary pairing QR code displayed by the sending device. Images from the camera are processed instantaneously in-memory on your device and are never saved or sent anywhere.
  • Photo Library / Storage Access: Used exclusively when you explicitly select photos, videos, or files you want to share, and to automatically save incoming media to your device's Camera Roll / Photos app.

4. Security & Cryptographic Integrity

Direct local socket connections utilize temporary, one-time authentication tokens generated during pairing. Transfers incorporate SHA-256 cryptographic checksums for each chunk, ensuring that your files cannot be corrupted or tampered with in-transit.

5. Web Receiver Beam & Browser Streaming

When you choose to beam files to a browser (e.g. computer or secondary device) via QR code, AnySharePro runs a temporary micro-HTTP daemon locally on your device. The receiving browser connects directly to your phone's local private IP address. No internet connection or cloud relay is ever engaged.

6. Children's Privacy (COPPA Compliance)

Because AnySharePro does not collect any personal data whatsoever, it is 100% compliant with the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA) and international child privacy regulations. It is completely safe for users of all ages.
