Data Protection & Privacy

🛡️ Privacy Policy

Last Updated: November 2026 • Effective Date: November 1, 2026 • Version 1.0.0

Summary: 100% Zero Data Collection Policy

AnySharePro is engineered with privacy as a foundational principle. We do not collect, process, log, track, or share any personal information or file data. All file transfers occur strictly peer-to-peer across your private local Wi-Fi or device hotspot. No files or metadata ever pass through or touch remote cloud servers.

1. Information We Do Not Collect

Because AnySharePro works 100% offline via direct device-to-device sockets, we operate with a strict zero-knowledge architecture:

  • No Personal Identifiers: We do not require account registration, login, email addresses, phone numbers, or passwords.
  • No File or Content Tracking: The files, photos, videos, and documents you send are streamed directly to the recipient over local TCP sockets. We have zero visibility into your file names, sizes, or contents.
  • No Analytics or Tracking SDKs: There are no advertising identifiers, third-party analytics trackers, or user telemetry libraries embedded in the application.
  • No Transfer Logs: Session data, device pairings, and transfer progress reside purely in temporary device RAM and are cleared upon app close.

2. Device Permissions & Why They Are Needed

AnySharePro requests only the minimum iOS/Android system permissions required to establish local connections and select files you wish to send:

  • Camera: Used exclusively to scan device-pairing QR codes. Camera video frames are processed in real-time on your device using native Apple Vision / Barcode scanner APIs and are never recorded or stored.
  • Photo Library & Files: Accessed only when you explicitly select photos, videos, or documents to send, or choose to save received media into your device's Camera Roll.
  • Local Network (iOS): Required by iOS to broadcast and listen on local network sockets (port 8080) for discovery and high-speed data transmission between devices connected to the same Wi-Fi or Personal Hotspot.

3. End-to-End Cryptography & Data Integrity

Every direct transfer session generates an ephemeral session token exchanged during the QR pairing handshake. Binary chunks are verified using hardware-accelerated SHA-256 cryptographic checksums, guaranteeing that your files are transmitted without packet alteration or corruption.

4. Third-Party Services & Links

AnySharePro does not bundle third-party ad networks, social logins, or behavioral tracking SDKs. Any external links in this documentation point exclusively to this official GitHub-hosted repository for user reference.

5. Children's Privacy (COPPA Compliance)

AnySharePro does not collect personal information from any user, including children under the age of 13. The application is completely safe for all audiences.

Assistance & FAQ

💬 Support & FAQ

Direct Developer Support & Troubleshooting Resources
Q: Does AnySharePro require an active Internet connection?
No. AnySharePro operates 100% offline using your local Wi-Fi network or Personal Hotspot. Your mobile data or internet bandwidth is never consumed.
Q: How do I connect an Android phone or PC to an iPhone?
Connect both devices to the same Wi-Fi router or turn on the iPhone's Personal Hotspot. Open AnySharePro, tap Send, and scan the displayed QR code with your Android phone's camera or browser.
Q: Why does iOS ask for "Local Network" permission?
iOS requires explicit user consent for apps to establish direct socket connections (HTTP/TCP) with other devices on your home or office Wi-Fi network.
