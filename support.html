Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

1. How do I achieve the fastest transfer speeds?
Make sure both devices are connected to the same 5GHz Wi-Fi network, or have one device turn on its Personal Hotspot and connect the other device to it. This unlocks raw physical hardware Wi-Fi speeds exceeding 50 to 100+ MB/s.
2. Why does the receiver say "Cannot Open Page" in Safari?
Both devices MUST be connected to the exact same Wi-Fi network or Personal Hotspot. If one device is on Cellular 5G/LTE and the other is on home Wi-Fi, they cannot communicate peer-to-peer.
3. Does the receiver need the app installed?
No! AnySharePro includes a "Web Receiver Beam" feature. The sender displays a QR code, and any phone, laptop, or tablet browser can scan it and download the files directly.
4. Where are received photos and videos saved?
Received photos and videos are automatically saved directly into your device's Camera Roll / Photos library with Smart Auto-Save. Documents and other archives are saved to your Downloads/Files storage.
5. Why is "Local Network" permission needed on iOS?
iOS 14+ requires explicit permission for apps to discover other devices on your local Wi-Fi router. AnySharePro uses this exclusively to locate nearby transfer peers and stream data directly.
